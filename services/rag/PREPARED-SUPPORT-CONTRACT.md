## Preparation contract

1. Keep every hash-bound canonical original unit, with immutable chunk identity and UTF-16 offsets. Preparation binds to the exact source generation, extraction manifest, structure manifest and audience. No published source content is rewritten.
2. An approved model proposes coherent workflow/policy groups over these original units. Each group has discovery labels, complete original unit addresses, required group dependencies and explicit scope. Labels are derived discovery aids, never answer evidence. Every original unit must be accounted for. Complete table/list/FAQ units remain atomic.
3. An independent model pass checks the proposed grouping, locality and dependencies against every original source unit. Distant restrictions, contrary rules, prerequisites, unresolved references and alternative normal workflows must remain discoverable and in the required closure. Ambiguous locality retains a conservative full-document obligation. Failed verification cannot produce a READY artifact.
4. Freeze input/output/work/call limits before evaluation. Oversize sources use bounded complete partitions and all-partition verification. Preparation cannot silently drop a unit or claim a locality proof that was never checked.
5. Store the artifact separately from canonical sources, under a new schema-validated version and source-generation binding. Additive persistence belongs to the existing knowledge entities and requires a reversible migration. Preparation is automated provenance, not human policy approval. Publication/replacement/deletion/audience changes invalidate serving eligibility immediately.

## Serving contract

1. Fetch only current authorized prepared indexes. Unknown, absent or stale preparation retains the existing complete-document path; it does not imply that policy is absent. Never silently treat legacy DOCUMENT scope as LOCAL.
2. Compare the original question and bounded contextual intent with every admitted workflow descriptor, including global/uncertain obligations. A fixed heading name or cached answer is not the selector. Oversize catalogs must report bounded capacity or use the existing authorized retrieval path without pretending the omitted catalog was inspected.
3. Expand the selected verified dependency closure to complete ORIGINAL support units. Validate every source binding and use these units as the only policy authority for drafting.
4. Independently verify final claims and applicable coverage against original closure text and the verified index. A bounded additional-support round may retrieve a missed group; uncertain coverage fails closed. Generated findings never become canonical policy evidence.
5. Recheck role, audience, catalog epoch and all influencing source generations before release. Preserve exact citations, read-only permissions, cancellation and aggregate budgets. Customer serving remains unchanged until separately accepted.

## Required regression and acceptance evidence

- A restriction far from its main procedure remains in the selected closure.
- A rejected preparation verdict, missing original unit, fabricated address, broken dependency or stale source generation cannot serve.
- Failed preparation keeps the previous usable artifact; restart and duplicate completion are idempotent.
- Audience/role changes and replacement invalidate both discovery and original-source access.
- Broad orientation and unspecified payment/arrival modes retain materially different applicable workflows and payment conditions.
- Freeze the prepared corpus and evaluate natural broad questions, fresh holdouts, follow-ups, useful refusal, first-use latency and repeated latency. Record failed samples. Static tests and model verdicts alone do not certify answer quality.
